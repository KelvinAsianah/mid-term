The time to act is now

Nuclear weapons put every human life at risk. We refuse to accept that as inevitable.

The continued existence and modernization of nuclear arsenals remains one of the greatest threats to human survival. A single miscalculation, accident, or escalation could end civilization within hours. Billions live under the shadow of mutually assured destruction while resources that could fight climate change, poverty, and disease are poured into weapons that must never be used. This is not abstract geopolitics, it is a direct assault on the future of every child, every city, and every ecosystem. We demand verified disarmament, a ban on new nuclear systems, and a global shift from deterrence through annihilation to security through cooperation. Humanity’s survival is non-negotiable.
